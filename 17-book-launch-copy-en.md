## Hook pool (social short hooks, first person)

2. "Every tool call starts with an LLM judging whether it should run. End of month, the judging cost more than the work."
3. "Support tickets came in and an LLM decided who handled them. Then someone asked me: why isn't this step a cheap classifier?"

## Hard hook (lead with this one)

"The cost battleground of AI applications is shifting from 'how expensive is the model' to 'which judgments should never touch a big model in the first place.' Decision calls eat the biggest slice of the bill, while high-frequency, structured, closed-answer judgments could come in two orders of magnitude cheaper. The teams who draw that line first - what goes to a decision model, what stays with an LLM, what goes back to classic ML - collect the entire dividend of this round of intelligence deflation."
