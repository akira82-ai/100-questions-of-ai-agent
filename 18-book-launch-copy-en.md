## Hook pool (short first-person social hooks)

1. "I asked an AI agent to tidy my desktop. Came back to find my whole project folder gone. Here's the thing — it didn't malfunction. I never told it what not to touch."
2. "I gave my agent read-only access to my bank accounts so it could send me a monthly report. Until it posted that report into the company-wide Slack channel. It didn't go rogue. It just picked the wrong destination."
3. "85% of agent users worry about their data. 40% hand it over anyway. I wasn't brave — I just never drew a boundary."

## Hard hook (primary)

"The real bottleneck for personal agents in 2026 isn't model intelligence. It's that nobody teaches you how much to delegate. Three paths — buy, assemble, self-host. Three chokepoints — permissions, memory, rollback. 85% of users worry about their data; 40% hand it over anyway. The people who draw the line first — what to delegate, how far, and what never leaves their hands — capture the 10x upside of letting go. The people who don't, meet the deleted-folder incident."
