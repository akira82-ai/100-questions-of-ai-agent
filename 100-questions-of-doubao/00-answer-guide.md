# How to Read This Book

This is the English edition of *关于豆包工作的 100 个问题* (100 Questions about Doubao Work), book 15 of the 100 Questions series.

**What "Doubao Work" is.** Doubao Work is ByteDance's agent work platform — launched August 25, 2026 at `doubao.com/work`, positioned as a team AI coworker / digital employee. It breaks down real work goals into tasks and delivers documents, spreadsheets, slides, web pages and small apps; it can run scheduled tasks, operate your computer (locally or via cloud computer), and — when connected to Feishu — works with your team's knowledge under existing permissions. This book is strictly about the Doubao Work product; the Doubao app and Doubao's large models appear only as ecosystem context.

**How the answers were built.** Every answer was compiled from primary and secondary sources gathered on September 11, 2026: official pages (doubao.com/work, Feishu help center, ByteDance Seed blog), launch coverage, hands-on reviews (both glowing and critical), pricing analyses, and community threads (V2EX, X). The Chinese edition's working files keep the full source registry with URLs; this edition omits inline source lines for readability.

**Three things to keep in mind while reading:**

1. **Snapshot timing.** This product iterates weekly — skills numbered 106 in late August, "200+" in the side workbench; prices are ¥68/¥200/¥500 monthly for individuals and ¥166/seat/month (annual) for teams as of this snapshot. Treat every number as "as of September 2026."
2. **Predecessor vs. launch version.** Some failure records come from June 2026 tests of the earlier "Work Tasks" / professional-edition builds. Where the source dates matter, the text says so; treat those as history, not a verdict on the current product.
3. **Question numbers are shared with the Chinese edition** (including gaps where questions were merged during drafting), so the two editions can be read side by side.

**The five-chapter journey:** Chapter 1 decides whether it can do your work at all; Chapter 2 gets you through day one without accidents; Chapter 3 teaches you to verify when it fails; Chapter 4 shows the work you can genuinely hand over; Chapter 5 draws the line on how far to open it to your team.
